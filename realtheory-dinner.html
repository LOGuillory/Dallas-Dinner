Why now

AI is changing the economics of cloud faster than most teams are prepared for. A new wave of cost complexity has arrived — GPUs, token-based billing, workloads that behave very differently than traditional infrastructure.

At the same time, AI is beginning to reshape FinOps itself — moving teams beyond dashboards and spreadsheets toward systems that can reason about cost, performance, and risk.
